A Country of Culture & Faith

Nepal is a meeting point of Hindu and Buddhist traditions. Pashupatinath Temple, on the bank of the Bagmati River, is among the most sacred Shiva temples in the world and attracts devotees from across South Asia.

Festivals such as Dashain, Tihar and Holi fill the calendar with colour, music, food and family gatherings. Visitors are always welcomed with a warm "Namaste".

About Us

Who we are, what we believe in and why we love Nepal.

Our Story

Discover Nepal began as a student project with a simple idea: show the world the real Nepal, not only the famous peaks but also the temples, forests, lakes and people that make the country special.

We collect photos and information about Nepal's most beautiful places and present them in a clear, friendly way, so that anyone planning a visit, or just curious, can learn something new in a few minutes.

Our Mission & Values

Mission

Why Nepal?

Nepal is small but extremely diverse. Within a few hours you can move from tropical jungle at 60 metres above sea level to the snow-covered Himalayas above 8,000 metres. It is the birthplace of Siddhartha Gautama, the Buddha, in Lumbini.

The country has more than 120 languages and 125 ethnic groups, creating a rich mix of food, dress, music and festivals that you will not find anywhere else.
